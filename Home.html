LORE

Kosmic Misfits wasn’t created because I wanted to make another NFT project. It started because I finally built something that reflected how I’ve felt my whole life. Like I never quite fit the mold. The one who thought differently. The black sheep. The outcast. The one who didn’t quite belong anywhere. Then one day I stopped trying to fit into someone else’s world… …and started building my own. That’s how The Drift was born. A place where being different isn’t something to hide. It’s your greatest strength. I’m intentionally leaving room for The Drift to grow because I don’t want to dictate every piece of its story on day one. The people who discover Kosmic Misfits early won’t just watch it grow. They’ll help shape what it becomes. Because the best worlds aren’t built by one person. They’re built by the Misfits who finally found where they belong. Lost in Space. Found in the Drift.
